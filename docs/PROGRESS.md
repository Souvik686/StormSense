## 2. What has been completed

- **Data inventory**: `scripts/00_inventory.py` (13 KB) has been written and run;
  produced `reports/data_inventory.json` (~25 KB) cataloguing every raw source
  (ERA5, IMD rainfall, INSAT, lightning, radar, DEM, boundaries, normal samples).
- **Config system**: `configs/default.yaml` is fleshed out and loads cleanly via
  `src/utils/config.py` (verified `load_config()` runs without error). Encodes:
  - Domain: West Bengal + synoptic context, 20–28°N / 84–90°E, 0.25° ERA5 grid
    (33×25 cells).
  - ERA5 variable/pressure-level layout, with a documented caveat that
    u/v only exist at 1000/850/700 hPa while z/q/t exist at 700/500/300/250 hPa.
  - INSAT explicitly flagged `use_for_training: false` — only 93 hours of overlap
    with ERA5 (2 cyclone events), kept for qualitative case-study visuals only.
  - Label thresholds for heavy/extreme rain and severe CAPE/CIN, derived from
    empirical ERA5 tp distribution (~p99.3 / p99.9 / p88).
  - 6-hour input window, multi-horizon targets at lead times 2–6 h, chronological
    train/val/test split by year (2021–22 / 2023 / 2024) with a 6h leakage buffer.
- **Data pipeline source modules** (all written, not yet verified end-to-end):
  `era5_loader.py`, `dem_loader.py`, `preprocess.py`, `windowing.py`, `dataset.py`,
  plus feature modules `normalize.py` and `targets.py`.
- **Model source modules**: `baseline.py` and `advanced.py` written (not yet

- **Training utility modules**: `losses.py` and `metrics.py` written.

  (~1.0 GB, generated 2026-09-08 12:13) — this is the merged pressure+single-level
